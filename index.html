The intention behind Noor

A gentle way back to the words that matter.

Noor is a simple Quranic resource designed to help people turn to the Qur’an during different moments in life — whether they are seeking comfort, hope, forgiveness, patience, gratitude, guidance, or a reminder of the Hereafter.

Arabic text and English translations are presented separately. Translations are interpretations of meaning; consult the Qur’an and trusted scholarship for fuller context.
